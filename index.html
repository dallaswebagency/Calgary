Do Tenants Have To Clean When Moving Out?

Most lease agreements include details about the condition the apartment should be left in on departure. In the vast majority of cases, the tenant is responsible for ensuring it is clean, either themselves or by hiring a professional team.

If your landlord does not provide the service, then you, as the tenant, should arrange the cleaning unless your landlord expressly dictates otherwise.

Can A Landlord Charge You For Cleaning After You Move Out?

Sometimes, usually when the house is left in an unacceptable condition. A landlord can use the security deposit to cover:

  • The cost of hiring a cleaning team if the place is left in a mess
  • Repairs to the house that resulted from your actions
  • Repainting discolored or stained walls (if it is not normal wear and tear)

They cannot use your security deposit or charge extra for general wear and tear. Move Out Cleaners can help.

Should A House Be Deep Cleaned Before Moving In?

It is always best to arrange a deep cleaning service beforehand. It is beneficial for hygiene, safety, and the overall experience for the new arrivals: a completely fresh slate, protection for your furniture and belongings from old mess, and more comfort right from the get-go.

How Clean Should Your House Be When You Sell It?

Buyers want to envision themselves living there, which is much easier when the home is beautifully clean and free of odour. Once the house sells, have it deep-cleaned at least once before the new owners arrive.

What Is The Difference Between Basic Cleaning And Deep Cleaning?

A deep clean is everything a basic clean is, plus all the extras that often get missed: on top of cabinets, inside cupboards, around doorframes, and the vents. It includes thorough disinfection and sanitization of all your appliances and surfaces.

How Often Should I Schedule A Deep Clean For My Home?

Annually at the very least. In our expert opinion, twice a year is far better, or once per season if you feel your home needs it. The benefits include less dust build up, a more consistent level of cleanliness, a party and visitor ready home, and upkeep of property quality and value.
